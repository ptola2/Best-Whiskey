# Waterline Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 13 October 2026**

Published at: https://malebolabs.com/waterline-privacy-policy/

This Privacy Policy explains how Waterline ("Waterline", "we", "us") handles your information when you use the Waterline iOS app. Waterline is made by Malebo Labs and operated by Pasito Tola, who is the data controller for any personal data described here. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the app.

Contact for any privacy question or request: **passytotola@gmail.com**

## In short

- Waterline works without an account. What you enter stays on your iPhone, in a file that iOS encrypts and only Waterline can read.
- We cannot see your entries. Nothing leaves your iPhone unless you choose to turn on Backup and sync.
- If you connect Apple Health, Waterline reads and saves weight and blood pressure there, on your iPhone, only with your permission.
- There are no ads, no analytics tools, no tracking and no AI services in Waterline.
- We never sell your information, and we never use your health information for advertising or marketing.
- You can delete everything from inside the app at any time.
- Waterline is an educational tool, not a medical device. Please read section 13 before you fast.

## 2. Your health information

Most of what you enter in Waterline is health information. Privacy laws treat it as sensitive (special category data under the UK GDPR and EU GDPR), and so do we:

- We never use it for advertising, marketing or data mining.
- We never sell it, rent it or share it with data brokers or information resellers.
- We never use it to make decisions about you, such as for credit, insurance or employment.
- We never use it to train AI models.
- We never share it with third parties, except the backup provider in section 4 and only if you turn backup on.

**Apple Health (optional).** Waterline uses Apple Health only if you turn it on in Profile. iOS then asks your permission, and you choose exactly what Waterline may read and save:

- **Read:** weight, blood pressure and heart rate, to fill in your check-ins and profile when you tap Use. Readings from other apps and devices, such as a smart scale or a blood pressure cuff, are shown only on your iPhone.
- **Save:** the weight and blood pressure from your check-ins, so they sit with your other health records.
- Data from Apple Health is never used for advertising, marketing or data mining, never sold, and never shared with third parties. It is not sent to us.
- If you also turn on Backup and sync, the check-ins you save are backed up as section 4 describes, including any value you took from Apple Health.
- Removing a check-in in Waterline also removes what Waterline saved to Apple Health for it. Delete all my data does not change Apple Health: you can delete readings in the Health app.
- You can change or withdraw Waterline's access at any time in the Health app: tap your profile picture, then Apps, then Waterline.

## 3. Information kept on your iPhone

By default, all of the information in section 1 is saved only on your iPhone, inside Waterline's private storage. iOS encrypts it with data protection, and it cannot be read until you unlock your iPhone after it restarts. We do not have a copy and cannot see it.

Your iPhone's own backups (iCloud Backup or a backup to your computer) may include Waterline's data along with your other apps. You and Apple control those backups under your Apple ID, and Apple's privacy policy applies to them.

## 4. Backup and sync (optional)

Backup and sync is optional, and Waterline works fully without it. If you turn it on in Profile:

- **Sign-in.** You sign in with Apple, and the sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication. We ask Apple only for your email address, not your name. If you choose Hide My Email, we receive a private relay address instead.
- **What is stored.** A copy of your Waterline data (everything listed in section 1) is stored in Google Cloud Firestore, linked to your account identifier, with a revision number and timestamp used to keep your devices in step.
- **Who can read it.** Access rules allow only your own signed-in account to read or change your copy. Google encrypts it in transit and at rest.
- **Sync.** Your copy is kept in step across iPhones signed in to the same account.
- **Signing out.** Waterline backs up any pending changes, then removes your data from that iPhone. Your copy stays in your account until you delete it.
- **Delete all my data.** Removes your data from that iPhone and clears your cloud copy, so your other signed-in iPhones clear too. If the cloud copy cannot be cleared, nothing is deleted and the app tells you.
- **Delete account.** After you confirm with Apple, Waterline revokes its access to your Apple ID, deletes your cloud copy and then deletes your account. The data on that iPhone stays until you remove it with Delete all my data or delete the app.

We rely on your explicit consent to store your health information in the cloud. You give it by turning on Backup and sync, and you can withdraw it at any time by deleting your account.

## 5. Notifications

Reminders (check-in prompts, milestones and safety alerts) are scheduled by iOS on your iPhone. They are not sent from a server, and Waterline does not create a push notification token.

Because reminders can mention your fast, they may appear on your Lock Screen. You can hide notification previews in iOS Settings, or turn reminders off in Waterline's Profile tab.

The Waterline widget and Live Activity show how long you have been fasting, your current phase and your progress on the Home Screen, the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, where others near your iPhone can see them. They read this from a shared space on your iPhone that only Waterline and its widget can use; nothing is sent anywhere. You can remove the widget, and turn Live Activities off in iOS Settings, Waterline.

## 6. Links and phone calls

- The Learn section links to published research on other websites, such as PubMed Central, MDPI and Karger. Links open in your browser, and those websites have their own privacy policies. Waterline does not send them any of your information.
- The Call button opens the Phone app with the number you entered for your clinician. The call is handled by iOS and your phone carrier, and Waterline does not record it.

## 8. How long we keep information

- **On your iPhone:** until you delete it with Delete all my data, remove individual check-ins, or delete the app.
- **In the cloud (Backup and sync only):** until you clear it with Delete all my data or delete your account. Deleted data is removed from our active database straight away. Google may hold it in encrypted backups for a limited period before it is overwritten.
- **Emails you send us:** for as long as needed to answer you, and no longer than 2 years.

If you cannot open the app, email us from the address linked to your account and we will delete your cloud copy and account within 30 days.

## 10. Security

Data on your iPhone is encrypted by iOS data protection. If you use Backup and sync, your data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS), Google encrypts it at rest, and access rules limit your copy to your own account.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the relevant authorities where the law requires it.

## 13. Health and safety notice

Please read this before you use Waterline.

- **Not medical advice.** Waterline is an educational tool built from published research on water-only fasting. It is not a medical device, and it does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition. It does not replace a doctor.
- **Talk to a doctor first.** See a doctor before any water fast, especially if you have a medical condition or take any medicine. Never stop or change a medicine without your doctor.
- **Supervision.** The research Waterline is built on restricts fasts longer than 72 hours to medically supervised settings. Waterline asks you to confirm that a clinician is supervising before it will plan a longer fast, and it warns you if an unsupervised fast passes 72 hours.
- **Who should not fast.** Water fasting is not safe for anyone under 18, during pregnancy or breastfeeding, or for people with type 1 diabetes, a current or past eating disorder, advanced kidney or liver disease, acute porphyria, a BMI under 18.5, or severe weight or muscle loss. Waterline's safety check blocks these, but it relies on your answers.
- **Limits of the app.** Check-in results come from published thresholds and simple rules. They can miss a real problem or flag a harmless one, and they are only as accurate as the measurements you enter. Waterline does not monitor you continuously, and because we do not receive your data, nobody is alerted if something goes wrong.
- **Refeeding.** Breaking a long fast too quickly can cause refeeding syndrome, which can be life-threatening. Follow your clinician's refeeding plan. The plan in the app is general guidance.
- **Stop and get help.** End the fast and contact a doctor straight away if you faint or nearly faint, have chest pain or an irregular heartbeat, become confused, cannot keep water down, start fast deep breathing, get a hot swollen joint, or notice swelling or shortness of breath while refeeding.
- **Emergencies.** Waterline is not an emergency service. In an emergency, call your local emergency number, such as 999 in the UK, 112 in the EU or 911 in the US.
- **Eating difficulties.** If food, weight or eating feel hard to manage, please talk to your doctor or a local eating disorder support service before fasting.
