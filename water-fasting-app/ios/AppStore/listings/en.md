## Description

_1899 characters_

```
Waterline helps you understand and track a water-only fast, built from what the clinical research says, including the risks.

KNOW WHERE YOU ARE
• A live timer shows each metabolic phase, from glycogen running out to the 72-hour threshold.
• A timeline and a "watch for" card show which problems usually start on which day.

CHECK IN TWICE A DAY
• Log weight, ketones, lying and standing blood pressure, pulse and symptoms.
• Each check-in is graded against published thresholds, so you can see which signs need attention and which mean stop and get help.
• Track plain water against the 1.2 to 2.5 L daily range.

SAFETY FIRST
• A safety check built from the medical contraindications rules out water fasting for people it does not suit, such as type 1 diabetes, pregnancy or an eating disorder.
• Medicines and conditions that need a doctor are flagged before you start.
• As in the research, fasts longer than 72 hours need confirmed medical supervision.
• Add your clinician's phone number so it is always one tap away.

BREAK THE FAST SAFELY
• A refeeding plan sized to your fast, using the ASPEN and NICE risk levels: how long it lasts, how many calories, what to eat each day and what to watch for.

LEARN THE SCIENCE
• Hour-by-hour changes, hormones, study results, side effects and refeeding syndrome, with a link to every source.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account needed. Everything stays on your iPhone. No ads and no tracking.

IN YOUR LANGUAGE
• 21 languages, including Arabic, Chinese, French, German, Hindi, Japanese, Spanish and Swahili. Switch at any time.

IMPORTANT
Waterline is an educational tool. It is not a medical device and not medical advice, and it does not diagnose or treat any condition. Water fasting is not safe for everyone. See a doctor before any fast, and fast longer than 72 hours only under medical supervision. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
```
