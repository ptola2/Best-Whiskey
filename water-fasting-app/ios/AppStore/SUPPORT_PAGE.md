## Waterline support

Waterline is a companion app for water-only fasting, built from published clinical research. If something is not
working or you have a question, email **passytotola@gmail.com**. We usually reply within two working days.

**In an emergency, call your local emergency number. Waterline cannot give medical help.**

### Questions

**Is Waterline medical advice?**
No. Waterline is an educational tool. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition.
See a doctor before any fast, and fast longer than 72 hours only under medical supervision.

**Why can't I start a fast?**
The safety check in Profile rules out water fasting when an answer matches a medical reason not to fast, such as
type 1 diabetes, pregnancy, an eating disorder, a BMI under 18.5 or being under 18. If an answer was a mistake,
change it in Profile. If it was not, please talk to your doctor about what is safe for you.

**Why does a fast longer than 72 hours ask about supervision?**
The research on fasts that long comes from clinics with twice-daily checks and blood tests. Waterline asks you to
confirm that a clinician is supervising before it plans a fast beyond 72 hours.

**Where is my data stored?**
On your iPhone, in a protected file only Waterline can read. Nothing is sent anywhere. Read the full
[privacy policy](https://malebolabs.com/waterline-privacy-policy/).

**How do I delete my data?**
Profile, Your data, Delete all my data. Deleting the app also removes everything Waterline stores. Readings it saved to
Apple Health stay there until you delete them in the Health app.

**How do I change the language?**
Tap the flag at the top of Today or Profile, or use the Language card in Profile. Choose "Same as iPhone" to follow
your phone's language. Waterline is available in 21 languages.

**How does Apple Health work with Waterline?**
Turn on Apple Health in Profile and allow access. Waterline then saves the weight and blood pressure from your
check-ins to Health, and the check-in form shows recent readings from a smart scale or blood pressure cuff so you can
use them with one tap. To change access later, open the Health app, tap your profile picture, then Apps, then
Waterline.

**How do I add the widget?**
On the Home Screen, touch and hold an empty area, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and choose Waterline. While a fast runs,
the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island also show it as a Live Activity. iOS ends a Live Activity after about 8 hours;
opening Waterline starts a new one. Live Activities can be turned off in Settings, Waterline.

**Reminders are not arriving.**
Check that Reminders is on in Profile, and that notifications are allowed for Waterline in the iPhone's Settings,
Notifications. Reminders run while a fast is in progress.

**Can I use Waterline on more than one device?**
Not in this version: your data stays on the iPhone where you entered it.
